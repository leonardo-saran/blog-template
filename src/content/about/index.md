This is the template About page, published as the demo bio. Replace it with your own story when you fork the template.

The site is a simple static blog and portfolio. It is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript - no frameworks, no dependencies, and no backend - so it is fast, secure, and costs nothing to host.

This page is your chance to introduce yourself to visitors and recruiters:

- Who you are, in one sentence
- What you're working on now
- A link or two: your email, your GitHub, your LinkedIn

The content index and site files regenerate automatically from the settings, so publishing is as easy as writing a Markdown file and pushing a commit.

You can add your profile photo at src/assets/profile.jpg. A square image around 400 by 400 pixels looks best and will appear here automatically.
